SIR is a lightweight internet radio player for Android. Browse stations, start
playback with one tap, and control audio from the notification, lock screen,
Android Auto, or a Wear OS companion.

Features:
• Listen to internet radio stations with reliable background playback
• Browse and search station directories, and save favorites
• Use media controls from notifications, the lock screen, and Android Auto
• Set a sleep timer and adjust the equalizer
• See track information when a station provides it
• Optional Chromecast support is available in the separate Play flavor; it is
  not included in this FOSS build

Privacy:
• No account or sign-in is required
• Preferences are stored on your device
• The FOSS build does not include Firebase analytics or crash reporting
• Network access is used for station directories, radio streams, and artwork
